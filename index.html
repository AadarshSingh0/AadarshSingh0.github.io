About

I am a PhD student in theoretical particle physics at the Centre for High Energy Physics, Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, working with Prof. Sudhir K. Vempati. I expect to defend my thesis in November 2026, and I am looking for postdoctoral positions starting in 2027.

My research is on physics beyond the Standard Model: where the hierarchies in fermion masses and mixing come from, what effective field theory tells us about new neutrino interactions, and computational and AI methods for particle physics.

Research

Flavour physics from mass chains

Why are fermion masses so different from each other, and why is lepton mixing so different from quark mixing? I study mass chain models, such as clockwork, and their generalizations. My work includes neutrino masses in clockwork models, a rank-preserving framework that controls left- and right-handed zero modes independently, fractal mass chains that reproduce the lepton masses and mixing with only a few parameters (with A. Ibarra and S. K. Vempati), and random couplings in theory space.

AI and computational physics

I developed a Fourier method for finding tunnelling paths in multi-field vacuum decay, with S. R. Kannagi and S. K. Vempati. I also built HEPToolBench, a benchmark that tests how reliably language models drive standard collider-simulation software, and HEPLocalAgent, which runs validated MadGraph, Pythia and Delphes workflows from plain-language requests on a local computer. These show that language models, including small local models, do much better when they work through a structured interface with automatic checks.

Neutrinos, dark matter and astroparticle physics

With G. D'Ambrosio and S. K. Vempati, I derived theoretical and experimental constraints on four-lepton SMEFT operators in the μ–τ sector. Within dimension-six SMEFT, they exclude heavy-mediator completions of strong νμ–νμ and νμ–ντ self-interactions, while light mediators remain open. I also work on geometric phases in neutrino oscillations and on neutrino–dark matter interactions.
